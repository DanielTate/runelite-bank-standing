# Changelog

## 1.1.1

- Fixed the Sit emote not adding "Take a seat" to the collection log.

## 1.1

The collection log is much bigger. Some of what's new is hidden on purpose: please don't
spoil it for other players.

### Collection log
- New **Gear** tab: 13 sets of tools and gear (axes, pickaxes, harpoons, defenders,
  godswords, partyhats, slayer helmets and more), each collected by standing at the right
  guild or bank with it. Hover a missing one to see where.
- New **Unlocks** tab, listing everything from the skill guide.
- New **Moments** tab: holidays, cats and dogs that keep you company while you stand, and
  dozens of moments across Calendar, Streaks, Luck, Crowds, Worlds and more.
- More drops: pocket junk for early luck, a five-piece banker's outfit, seasonal drops that
  only appear on their holiday, and a second, very rare pet.
- The hitsplats you've seen are collected, using the game's own splat art.
- Counts of the secret phrases and other hidden things you've found, grouped by kind. The
  log never says which ones.
- A **Collection log** link in the skill guide, so it's easy to find.

### Other
- More secrets to discover.
- Banks inside instances, like clan halls, are now recognised.
