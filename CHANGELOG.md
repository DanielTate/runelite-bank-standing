# Changelog

## 1.6

- A **Skill guide** button in the side panel opens the skill guide, so you don't need the
  skill tile to get to it.
- A **Show skill tile** checkbox in the side panel hides or shows the skill tile. It's also
  in the settings.
- Peeks last much longer: 5 seconds at level 5, 10 at 10, 20 at 20, 30 at 30, 45 at 50,
  a minute at 60, 2 minutes at 70 and 5 minutes at 85.
- New at 99, **Make yourself at home**: keep the bank open as long as you like. The stand
  never ends and the timer doesn't show.

## 1.5

### The Fractured Archive
- The two bank chests in the Fractured Archive's lobby are in the collection log, west and
  east as separate banks. Time already stood there moves to the east bank.
- Archive junk: three things that only turn up in your pockets at the Archive.
- New moments for the Archive, including one for standing there on its anniversary, 20 October.
- The banker on the east chest has things to say about the place, and calls across to the
  west chest too.
- More secrets to find down there.

### Tea
- At level 85, instead of a giant cup appearing beside you, a banker now walks over and hands
  you your tea. Now and then it's a banker from another bank you've stood at.

### Peeking in the bank
- Eight peeks instead of three, starting much sooner: open the bank for 1 second at level 5,
  rising to 30 seconds at level 85, without ending your stand.

### Skill guide and log
- Every line of the skill guide has its own icon: better boots as your reach grows, clocks as
  XP starts sooner, rugs in their real colours, and something fitting for each title.
- The bank rat pet looks like a rat, and now joins you while you stand: a rat of solid gold
  that scurries off for a look round now and then, leaving a trail of coins and jewellery.
- The moneybag pet hops around you every so often, flinging coins as it goes.
- A new pet, a gold butterfly, a little more common than the others. It flutters round you
  while you stand.
- Click a pet you own in the collection log to put it away, or bring it back out.
- The gold carpet at 99 comes with coin piles and gold bars.

### Side panel
- A "What's new" link next to the version number opens this changelog. It's marked NEW
  after an update until you've opened it.

### Fixes
- You no longer fidget while sitting on the stool or the throne.

## 1.4.1

- Progress syncing between computers is safer. If two copies of your progress end up out of
  step, for example from having RuneLite open on two computers at once, Bank Standing now
  combines them instead of keeping whichever saved last: the copy with the most XP, plus
  any collection log entries, phrases and journal entries found only on the other.
- The README now explains that your progress follows your RuneLite account when RuneLite
  syncs your settings.

## 1.4

### Active modifiers
- A small row of boxes in the corner shows what's helping your stand right now: the emote
  you just did with its countdown, and full outfits and other sets while they count. Hover
  one to see what it does.
- Sitting down and the like show how long until they're ready again, and a gold box adds up
  everything shown.
- Hidden ones only appear once you've found them. Turn it all off with "Show active
  modifiers" in the settings.

### Chat commands
- Type `!bs` (or `!bankstanding`) in chat for your level, XP and time stood.
- Add a word for more: `here`, a bank's name, `fav`, `regulars`, `banks`, `log`, `pet`,
  `secrets`, `session`, `streak` or `title`. `!bs help` lists them.
- Only you see the answer: your message is sent as typed, and your own copy of it is
  redrawn, like RuneLite's `!kc`.
- Each bank now counts your visits and when you first stood there, from this version on.

### Other
- Thanks to Hochep, now in the credits.

## 1.3.1

- Finding a secret phrase for the first time now shows the collection log popup, like
  everything else in the log.
- A phrase still counts with punctuation on the end, like a question mark.
- Phrases now count as soon as you're standing still at a bank, including the 30 seconds
  before your first drop.

## 1.3

Plenty more to find. As always, please don't spoil the secrets for other players.

### Journal
- Once you've found some secret phrases, or been "Dressed for it", a little book appears next
  to their count in the collection log. Click it to read the ones you've found, with where
  and when you found them. New finds get a gold star until you've read them.
- Phrases you found before 1.3 get written into the journal the next time you say them.

### Skill unlocks
- Peeking in the bank now comes in steps: a Glance at level 40, a Quick peek at 50 and a Peek
  at 65.
- From level 85, bankers sometimes bring you a cup of tea.
- Two new titles, at levels 45 and 90.

### Other
- Lots more secret phrases, spread across almost every bank, plus a few other new secrets.
- A new hidden entry on the collection log's Gear tab.
- Sangvesti is now listed under Other realms in the collection log.

## 1.2.1

- Tooltips in the collection log show on top of it again, instead of underneath.
- The collection log's bottom and left borders now match the game's.
- The collection log's tab labels use the smaller font so they all fit, and the list on
  the left is centred in its highlight.

## 1.2

Lots more to find this time, and most of it is secret. Please don't spoil it for other players.

### Collection log
- Secret entries: some moments, and one drop, show as "Secret" with only a hint until you
  find them.
- A new **Places** entry on the Moments tab, full of secrets.
- A new gear set: **Random event outfits**. Wear the whole outfit while standing at any bank.
- Tutorial Island is no longer listed on the Banks tab.

### Other
- More secret phrases and other hidden things to discover.
- The version number now shows at the bottom of the side panel.
- The README has screenshots and a short FAQ.

### Fixes
- The collection log and skill guide are now drawn on top of the side panel and other
  plugins' bars in resizable mode.
- The skill guide always opens on its first tab.
- The worn rug (levels 10 to 39) no longer has a gap in the middle.
- "Today" in the side panel, and everything else that counts days, now follows your own
  clock. It used to roll over at midnight UTC.
- Progress is now saved when you close RuneLite, and a world hop no longer resets your
  session stats.
- If your saved progress ever can't be read, a copy of it is kept instead of being
  overwritten.
- Right-clicking in the collection log or skill guide no longer counts as a left click, and
  a closed window can no longer block clicks.
- Banks two tiles wide, like the Bank Camel at the Tombs of Amascut, are now measured from
  the tiles they really stand on. Before, some couldn't be reached at low levels.
- Fixed a few rare crashes when turning the plugin off or clicking in the collection log.

## 1.1.1

- Quick peek now shows a stopwatch above the bank, counting down how long you can keep it
  open before your stand ends.
- Fixed the Sit emote not adding "Take a seat" to the collection log.
- The max hit splat now only counts for the collection log once your max hit is 10 or more
  (level 19). At low levels it was coming up every other drop.
- The 6 hour stand milestone can now actually be earned. The game logs you out at 6 hours,
  so it used to be impossible.

## 1.1

The collection log is much bigger. Some of what's new is hidden on purpose: please don't
spoil it for other players.

### Collection log
- New **Gear** tab: 13 sets of tools and gear (axes, pickaxes, harpoons, defenders,
  godswords, partyhats, slayer helmets and more), each collected by standing at the right
  guild or bank with it. Hover a missing one to see where.
- New **Unlocks** tab, listing everything from the skill guide.
- New **Moments** tab: holidays, cats and dogs that keep you company while you stand, and
  dozens of moments across Calendar, Streaks, Luck, Crowds, Worlds and more.
- More drops: pocket junk for early luck, a five-piece banker's outfit, seasonal drops that
  only appear on their holiday, and a second, very rare pet.
- The hitsplats you've seen are collected, using the game's own splat art.
- Counts of the secret phrases and other hidden things you've found, grouped by kind. The
  log never says which ones.
- A **Collection log** link in the skill guide, so it's easy to find.

### Other
- More secrets to discover.
- Banks inside instances, like clan halls, are now recognised.
